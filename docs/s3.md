  execute unit stayed active after the companion process exited. The fixture
  unit was explicitly stopped. This candidate drains only execute workers,
  with one 15-second total grace after cancellation; filesystem workers remain
  bounded and may be abandoned. A grace timeout is an explicit shutdown error,
  not proof of cleanup. Actual user-service acceptance must be rerun after
  review and installation.

Final destructive revoke/uninstall has not been exercised on the live
enrollment. Re-enrollment requires a second owner-created grant; do not destroy
the only working enrollment and then claim it remains usable.
